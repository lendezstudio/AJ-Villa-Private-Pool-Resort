02The Experience

A closer look at AJ Villa

Browse actual property photos and see the spaces before making your inquiry.

The Pool

A long, tiled pool set right beside the villa, with a built-in ledge, a wood-look edge and a patterned deck with poolside seating. More recent photos show a shade canopy stretched over the water.

Living Room

A bright, double-height living room with marble-look floors, a long sofa and a wood-accented TV wall, crowned by a cascading ring pendant light. Tall windows look out toward the pool.

Kitchen & Dining

An open kitchen with white cabinetry and a marble-look breakfast bar lined with stools, opening onto a round dining table beneath modern pendant lights.

Lounge & Stairs

A second sitting area with warm wood-look floors, a sofa and a ceiling fan, overlooking the living room below. It's reached by a staircase with softly lit steps and glass railings.

Bedrooms

The photos show bedrooms with double beds and others with bunk beds, finished with crisp white linens and window blinds. Ask AJ Villa about the room setup that suits your group.

Bathrooms

Bathrooms finished in striped stone-look tile, with glass shower enclosures, dark vanity cabinets and backlit mirror cabinets.

Balcony

A covered balcony with a wood-panel wall and a dining set, opening to a wide view of fields and greenery. Another balcony looks straight down to the pool.

The Villa

A contemporary two-storey home with deep roof overhangs, wood-slat and charcoal panel cladding, and a walled, gated frontage lit with warm wall lights at dusk.
